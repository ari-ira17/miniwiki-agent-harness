# Roboto Variable

`Roboto-Variable.ttf` and `Roboto-Italic-Variable.ttf` are the upright and
italic variable fonts for Roboto, including Latin and Cyrillic glyphs. They
are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1;
the full license and copyright notice are in [OFL-Roboto.txt](OFL-Roboto.txt).

Sources: [Roboto upright](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/roboto/Roboto%5Bwdth,wght%5D.ttf), [Roboto italic](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/roboto/Roboto-Italic%5Bwdth,wght%5D.ttf).
